Subject: Direct simulation by the S combinator — companion manuscript

Dear Members of the S Combinator Challenge Committee,

Please consider “Direct Simulation of Turing Machines by Parallel Innermost S-Reduction” for the Wolfram S Combinator Challenge. It complements my earlier manuscript, “Universal Computation with the S Combinator under Parallel Innermost Reduction.” The earlier construction generates and checks candidate computation histories. This manuscript gives a direct simulation along a fixed deterministic evaluation path.

The initial S-term stores the transition table. At each simulated step, parallel innermost reduction distributes the current tape environment through the directory and constructs the next configuration inside the call that encoded its predecessor. A fixed finite-state reader uses two descriptor tags to follow the corresponding entry. The same reader works for every table over the fixed alphabets. It locates the successive configurations, even on infinite runs, reports nothing between checkpoints, and never influences which redexes are contracted.

The accompanying archive contains the Lean sources, the Nanoda verification record, a runnable example and the earlier manuscript (version 1.2.0). I directed the research. OpenAI Codex assisted extensively with proof development, formalization, testing and writing.
