Subject: A second solution to the Wolfram S Combinator Challenge

Dear Members of the S Combinator Challenge Committee,

Please consider my manuscript “Direct Simulation of Turing Machines by Parallel Innermost S-Reduction” as a second solution to the challenge. My earlier manuscript, “Universal Computation with the S Combinator under Parallel Innermost Reduction,” established input–output universality by generating and checking candidate computation histories.

This paper addresses a stricter reading of universality along a deterministic path. Under the same fixed parallel innermost strategy, S-reduction itself produces each successive configuration of a universal Turing machine inside the subtree encoding its predecessor. A fixed finite-state reader locates these configurations in order, even on infinite runs.

The proof was checked with Lean and Nanoda. I directed the research. OpenAI Codex assisted extensively with proof development, formalization, testing and writing.
